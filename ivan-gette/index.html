I — Current Exhibition

Tutto PassaAK Galerie, Gendarmenmarkt, Berlin · 9 Sep – 22 Nov 2026

Tutto Passa, Italian for “everything passes”, describes an attitude that sees change not as loss but as value.

Tutto Passa, Italian for “everything passes”, describes an attitude that sees change not as loss but as value.

The series comprises fifteen works that Ivan Gette created during a stay in Pietrasanta, Tuscany. Each one explores a single moment, shaped by countless influences and never repeated.

Gette paints intuitively and without filter, in the moment. He translates these moments into colour, not as depictions but as memories that live on within us. The works are deliberately left unexplained, leaving space for an open, honest encounter between painting and viewer.

A work is only completed by the person who owns it: each collector gives their painting its own title, binding it inseparably to themselves.

Time is the most valuable thing we have, and the one thing that cannot be bought. Tutto Passa preserves what remains: the memory of the moment.

IV — The artist

“I paint because words don’t go far enough. Colour does.”

Ivan Gette (b. 1988) is a Berlin-based abstract painter whose work grows out of constant movement: cities, encounters, nights that refuse to fade. He has developed a raw, singular language of gesture and colour that comes from lived experience rather than observation.

Exhibited at Parallel Vienna and Art Miami during Art Basel.
