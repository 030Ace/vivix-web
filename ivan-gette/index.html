Current exhibition

Solo exhibition · 9 Sep – 22 Nov 2026

Tutto Passa

AK Galerie, Gendarmenmarkt, Berlin

Tutto Passa is Italian for “everything passes”.

The series comprises fifteen paintings, made in Pietrasanta and Berlin in 2025 and 2026, each in a single session and without sketches.

Each collector gives their painting its own title.

The artist

Ivan Gette (b. 1988) is a Berlin-based abstract painter whose work grows out of constant movement: cities, encounters, nights that refuse to fade. He has developed a raw, singular language of gesture and color that comes from lived experience rather than observation.

Works in private collections in 10 countries. Exhibitions incl. Art Miami (2023), Parallel Vienna (2025), POSITIONS Berlin (2026).
