Official Press Introduction of TUTTO PASSA

An intimate introduction to Ivan Gette’s latest artistic series ahead of Berlin Art Week 2026.

Ahead of Berlin Art Week 2026, contemporary artist Ivan Gette and AK Galerie hosted an exclusive press gathering at China Club Berlin, offering selected media representatives a first introduction to Tutto Passa.

Set within the refined surroundings of one of Berlin’s distinguished private members’ clubs, the gathering brought together invited journalists and guests for an intimate presentation of the collaboration. The occasion provided an opportunity to explore the philosophy behind Gette’s latest series, an artistic reflection on time, memory, and the fleeting nature of individual moments.

The event marked the official introduction of Tutto Passa ahead of its solo exhibition at AK Galerie on Gendarmenmarkt and its presentation at POSITIONS Berlin Art Fair during Berlin Art Week.
