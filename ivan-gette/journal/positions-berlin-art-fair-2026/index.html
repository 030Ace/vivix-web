POSITIONS Berlin Art Fair 2026: Ivan Gette presented by AK Galerie during Berlin Art Week

Photo: Angela Regenbrecht Photography

Event
POSITIONS Berlin Art Fair – 13th Edition
Dates
September 10–13, 2026
Location
Tempelhof Airport, Hangars 5–6, Berlin, Germany
Gallery
AK Galerie
Featured Artist
Ivan Gette

From September 10–13, 2026, contemporary artist Ivan Gette exhibited his works in collaboration with AK Galerie at the 13th edition of POSITIONS Berlin Art Fair, held at the historic Tempelhof Airport as part of Berlin Art Week.

Bringing together 96 international galleries from 20 countries, POSITIONS Berlin has established itself as an important platform for contemporary and modern art, connecting artists, galleries, and collectors within Berlin’s international art scene.

For this edition, AK Galerie presented a dedicated booth featuring a curated selection of Ivan Gette’s artworks. The presentation offered collectors, curators, and art enthusiasts an opportunity to engage with his artistic practice within the distinctive setting of one of Berlin’s prominent art fairs.

The exhibition marked another milestone in Gette’s artistic journey, further strengthening his presence within the international contemporary art landscape.
