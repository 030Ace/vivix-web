13 October 2021

Presentation at Gentleman’s Circle, Berlin, 2021

Location
Gentleman’s Circle, Berlin

At the Gentleman’s Circle in Berlin, Ivan Gette presented an exhibition that invited visitors into his world of transformed memories and emotional storytelling.

Gette’s work is rooted in the value of lived moments, shaped through encounters, narratives and the potential for personal transformation. His chosen colors, elements and figures merge to deconstruct reality and reveal a surreal layer shaped by his own experiences. Using familiar, everyday materials to evoke alternative worlds, Ivan explores themes of love, passion and intervention in an effort to mirror the universe through his paintings.

Each piece documents intricate details with emotional depth, where order and disorder, perception and memory, and beauty and aesthetics continuously shift. Every color carries its own meaning, marking a specific experience within the work.
