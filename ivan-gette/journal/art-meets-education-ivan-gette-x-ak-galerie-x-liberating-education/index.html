Art Meets Education: Ivan Gette x AK Galerie x Liberating Education

Photo: Angela Regenbrecht Photography

An evening celebrating contemporary art, philanthropy, and a shared commitment to educational opportunity.

On October 16, contemporary artist Ivan Gette, AK Galerie, and Liberating Education came together to host Art Meets Education, a charity event dedicated to expanding access to education for disadvantaged young people.

Held at AK Galerie, the evening featured a curated selection of Ivan Gette’s artworks, bringing together art collectors, enthusiasts, and supporters of the initiative. At the heart of the collaboration was a shared mission: raising funds to support the development of new schools and learning facilities, creating greater educational opportunities for young people from underserved communities.

A charity raffle contributed to the fundraising efforts, reflecting the collective commitment to making a meaningful difference through art and philanthropy.

The evening highlighted the power of collaboration, bringing together artistic expression and social responsibility in support of lasting educational impact.
