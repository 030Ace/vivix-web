12 December 2019

Loud Strokes – Group exhibition, Audemars Piguet House, Munich, 2019

Location
Audemars Piguet House, Munich

On Thursday, 12 December 2019, the Audemars Piguet House in Munich hosted the exhibition “Loud Strokes” in collaboration with SLEEK.

Known for their longstanding engagement with the art world, Audemars Piguet presented works that reflect a personal and expressive interpretation of contemporary life. Among the featured artists were Ivan Gette, Vera Kochubey and Monteiro Hasse, each contributing their own dynamic approach to abstract and semi-abstract painting. Ivan’s work stood out for its energetic strokes and emotional clarity, aligning with the exhibition’s focus on bold, contemporary expression.

Guests enjoyed an evening of vibrant art supported by champagne from Ruinart, the world’s oldest champagne house, adding to the atmosphere of creativity and celebration.
