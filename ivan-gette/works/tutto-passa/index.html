Tutto Passa

Tutto Passa, Italian for “everything passes”, describes an attitude that sees change not as loss but as value.

The series comprises fifteen paintings, made during a stay in Pietrasanta, Tuscany. Thirteen of them are shown here.

Titles are the artist’s reference only; the collector names the work. Every work is offered by personal request.
